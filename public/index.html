04 / The studio

Small studio.
Large ear-to-idea ratio.

Bunfeefee Studios LLC is an independent digital media and technology studio. We make things that work, things that play, and things that look like they need a tiny nap.

Our work spans software, digital art, and creative production, with occasional detours into modding and small storytelling projects. We like thoughtful engineering and playful design. These are allowed to occupy the same room.

Explore Cloud Marks for our group-activity app, or follow our public projects on GitHub. The art department is right here, quietly becoming a bakery.
